# SkyEye

### AI-Powered Urban Infrastructure Intelligence

SkyEye converts aerial road video into actionable infrastructure intelligence by detecting, assessing and prioritizing civic hazards for timely intervention.

---

## Problem Statement

**Urban Civic Infrastructure:** Bengaluru recorded **41,150 potholes across its five city corporations by March 2026**, with **1,263 remaining unattended**, highlighting the challenge of efficiently assessing, prioritizing and responding to recurring road-infrastructure hazards alongside waterlogging, drainage overflow and surface risks.

---

## Our Approach

**Aerial Video → AI Detection → Severity Assessment → Prioritization → Action Tracking → Verification**

SkyEye bridges the gap between **detection and action** by converting continuous aerial video into prioritized, actionable infrastructure incidents.
